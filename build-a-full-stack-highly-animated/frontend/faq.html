GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered plainly.

Here is how EarthKind works, where support goes, and what to expect when you join a project or event.

Frequently asked

Still unsure about something? Our team is happy to help with the details that matter to you.

Where does my donation go?

Donations support project supplies, transport, field coordination, teaching materials, and local partner reimbursements. See our impact page for the latest progress.

Can I volunteer without experience?

Yes. Each opportunity lists the time, location, and skills needed. Most roles include a short orientation before the work begins.

How are projects selected?

We start with a request from a community partner, then agree on a practical outcome, a delivery plan, and a way to measure progress together.

Can my group partner with EarthKind?

We work with schools, resident groups, businesses, and local organizations. Send us your idea, location, and preferred timeline so we can route it to the right person.

Do you accept in-kind support?

Sometimes. We welcome useful materials, tools, and professional skills when they match an active project. Contact us before sending anything.

How quickly do you reply?

Most enquiries receive a response within two working days. Include your city and the project or program you are asking about to help us reply faster.

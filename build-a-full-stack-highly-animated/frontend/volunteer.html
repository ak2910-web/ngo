VOLUNTEER

Bring your time. We’ll bring the plan.

There is no perfect background required. Volunteers help with events, community visits, logistics, repair days, digital support, and more.

Weekend shiftsFlexible rolesTeam-based work

Ways to help

Pick the role that fits your schedule. Small commitments matter when they are consistent and well organized.

Event crew

Set up spaces, greet guests, guide activity flow, and help things run smoothly from start to finish.

  • Registration desk support
  • Guest guidance and crowd flow
  • End-of-event reset and cleanup

Field support

Join local visits, transport materials, and lend a hand where projects need extra people on the ground.

  • Material packing and loading
  • Site support and coordination
  • Photo and note capture

Skills help

Use design, writing, photography, admin, or technology skills to strengthen the work behind the scenes.

  • Design and social posts
  • Copy, reports, and documentation
  • Spreadsheet, data, and admin help

Volunteer commitment options

Choose the level of involvement that fits your week, then grow into a larger role if it feels right.

One-time help

Join a single event or field day when you have a free weekend and want to see the work firsthand.

Monthly support

Take one recurring shift each month and build familiarity with the team, the site, and the community.

Core volunteer

Work with us regularly on planning, execution, and follow-up for a program or event series.

Specialist help

Offer a skill like design, writing, logistics, or tech when the team needs focused support on a task.

Want to join? Use the contact page or the volunteer form on the home page and tell us what kind of work fits your week best.
